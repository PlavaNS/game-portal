Wall of Riddles

Nine chests. Nine gems. One wall.

In front of you is a wall with nine locked chests. Each one holds a gem, and some also hide a clue for another chest. Collect all nine gems and the wall is yours.

  • The three green chests can be solved right away.
  • Yellow and red chests need items you find along the way. Items drop into the bag at the bottom of the screen.
  • Tap an item to look at it. Drag it onto a chest to use it.
  • Stuck? Every chest has a hint button.
  • Best played by two or three people at one screen. Turn the sound up.
