Forever Dunk – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Forever Dunk is a web game made by Door 404. This policy explains what the game stores and why. Contact: contact@ilovewebgames.com.

1. Game progress

Your coins, unlocked skins, missions, records and settings are saved in your own browser (local storage). On CrazyGames they are saved with your CrazyGames account through the CrazyGames SDK. We do not receive this data.

2. Global leaderboard

The leaderboard is optional. If you enter a nickname, we store:

  • the nickname you choose (shown publicly on the leaderboard),
  • your best score of the day and of all time,
  • a random player ID created on your device (not linked to your identity),
  • a two-letter country code taken from your browser's language setting,
  • the date of your last score.

We do not ask for your real name, email or any account. The data is kept in our database hosted by Supabase and is used only to show the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname. To have your entry removed, email us with your nickname.

On CrazyGames the game uses the CrazyGames leaderboard and username instead; that data is handled by CrazyGames.

3. Advertising

Ads in the game are served by the website or platform where you play it (for example Google AdSense on ilovewebgames.com, CrazyGames, GameDistribution, GameMonetize or Playgama). These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, under their own privacy policies.

4. Children

The game is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a nickname, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete your leaderboard entry at any time at contact@ilovewebgames.com. Clearing your browser data removes the progress saved on your device.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
